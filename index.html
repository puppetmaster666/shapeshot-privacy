Shapeshot privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Shapeshot: Pool Runout Trainer, by puppetmaster666

What we collect

Nothing. Shapeshot does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, runs, ratings), settings and Pro Shop items are saved only on your phone, inside the app. They are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Accounts, ads and analytics

There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in this version. The app does not use the internet to play.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, this page will be updated to say what is collected and why, before the app does it.
